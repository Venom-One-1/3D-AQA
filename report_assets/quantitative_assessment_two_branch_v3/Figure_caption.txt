Quantitative Action Quality Assessment. Segmented SMPL sequences are assessed through two complementary branches. (a) Reference anchors sampled at 1 FPS are matched to student poses along an SMPL-DTW path; matched local-joint geodesic distances are averaged to obtain the pose deviation. (b) Body-size-normalized pelvis signals are temporally aligned and compared with a teacher reference profile. Calibrated horizontal-trajectory, endpoint, and vertical-trajectory deviations are combined into a pelvis deviation. Each branch ranks the same student cohort, and equal-weight Borda fusion produces the relative ranking. Blue and green consistently identify the pose and pelvis branches, respectively.

The meshes, correspondence examples, joint-distance heatmap, and lateral-pelvis curves reuse saved tracking and experiment outputs. Gold denotes the reference; in the pelvis plot it is the pointwise median of ten teachers. Orange denotes the illustrated student. Uniform sampling selects reference anchors only; DTW operates on the full sampled pose sequences. Anonymous ranking bars illustrate ordering, not measured ranks or absolute quality scores.
